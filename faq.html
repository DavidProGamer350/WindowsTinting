Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about window tinting

How long does tinting last?

Between 3 and 10 years depending on the type of film selected and aftercare. Nano Ceramic has the highest durability.

Can the tinting get wet after installation?

Yes, but we recommend waiting 48 hours after installation before exposing it to water. This allows the adhesive to cure properly.

Is tinting legal in Florida?

Yes, in Florida it is legal as long as the tinting allows at least 28% of light through the side windows of the driver and passenger. We install in compliance with all regulations.

Does tinting interfere with electronic devices?

No, Nano Ceramic tinting does not interfere with GPS signals, radio, cell phones or other electronic devices.

How do I care for my tinting?

Avoid cleaning the windows for the first 5 days. After that, use a mild cleaner without ammonia and a microfiber cloth. Do not use abrasive products.

How long does installation take?

Complete installation of a vehicle takes between 2 to 4 hours depending on the type of film and number of windows to tint.

Does tinting protect against UV rays?

Yes, all our types of tinting block up to 99% of UV rays, protecting your skin and the vehicle interior from premature aging.

Can I install tinting myself?

We don't recommend it. Professional installation guarantees a bubble-free, durable finish without defects. Our warranty only covers professional installations.
